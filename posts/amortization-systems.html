SAC

The constant amortization system (Sistema de Amortização Constante) divides the principal into equal parts and charges interest on the outstanding balance. The principal amortization is constant, obtained by dividing the financed amount by the term; interest and installments are decreasing.

Price

The constant installment system, also called the French system (Sistema Francês). It fixes the installment and lets the composition vary: at the beginning it is almost all interest, at the end it is almost all principal. Constant installment, decreasing interest and increasing amortization.

Bullet

This is the simplest system. Everything is paid at maturity.
