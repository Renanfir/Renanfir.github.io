Portfolio

This is when the fund becomes the investor itself and uses its own profits to invest in other investment funds, repos (compromissadas), Brazilian Treasury Selic-linked bonds (LFT) and bank deposit certificates (CDB). This is done to avoid leaving money sitting idle, since these amounts make a big difference.

Terms

  • Fundo DI — investment funds.
  • Repo (compromissada) — a very short-term loan to the bank, which gives the fund a public security in exchange.
  • Brazilian Treasury Selic-linked bonds (LFT) — a public security with a floating rate (pós-fixado) that yields exactly the Selic rate.
  • CDB — a loan to the bank; an interest-bearing investment.
