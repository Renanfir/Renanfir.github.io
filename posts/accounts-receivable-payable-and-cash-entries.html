Accounts receivable

Shows what each fund has to receive, such as assignments (cessões), cards (cartões) and manual entries, which are late payment interest and reimbursements.

Accounts payable

Shows what each fund has to pay, among them:

  • Assignment (cessão) — pay the assignor (cedente) for the borderô that was paid.
  • Redemption — pay the shareholder.
  • Taxes — DARF, IR/IOF.
  • Manual — administration fees, custody, audit, legal and rating.

The parties and the taxes behind these payments are explained in Custody, audit, rating, legal, DARF, IR and IOF.

All of them come out of the net worth of the fund, so they reduce the PU of all shareholders. Because of this, they appear in the quote calculation.

Cash entries

This is the register of the effective movement, which involves a lot of things beyond accounts payable and accounts receivable. It is an overview of the fund.
