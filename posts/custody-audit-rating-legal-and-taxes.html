Custodian

The custodian holds the assets and handles financial settlement. For an FIDC, the role goes beyond mere safekeeping: the custodian verifies the underlying collateral (checking that the assigned trade receivables actually exist and match the schedule of receivables), monitors asset movements, and is responsible for document storage.

Audit

An independent auditor reviews the annual financial statements and issues an opinion. This is mandatory under CVM regulations; the fund's balance sheet requires verification by an external party to attest to the accuracy of the figures.

Rating

A rating agency assigns a risk grade (AAA, AA, A, etc.). Obtaining a rating is practically mandatory for the fund, as institutional investors often cannot purchase securities that lack one.

Legal

The law firm that drafts and maintains the fund's regulations, assignment agreements and asset documentation.

DARF

Federal Revenue Collection Document. This is the payment slip used for federal taxes.

IR

Income Tax. This applies to the returns earned by the quota holder.

IOF

A tax levied on redemptions made within less than 30 days, designed to discourage short-term trading ("in-and-out" activity).
