Equity (patrimônio) is the side of the fund that deals with quotas instead of receivables: how many quotas the fund offers, and who buys them. Three records drive that: issuances, capital contributions and the investors behind them.

Issuances (emissões)

An issuance is the record of the quotas that the investment fund is selling. Each record has its own quota class — Senior, Mezzanine or Subordinated.

Issuance status

Status What it means
Draft (rascunho) being prepared, not issued yet
In distribution (em distribuição) offering in progress, raising capital
Active (ativa) issued and accruing returns
Closed (encerrada) settled or matured
Cancelled (cancelada) died before being issued

Terms

PU (preço unitário)

PU stands for unit price, and it is calculated this way:

PU = net worth / quantity of quotas

Capital contributions (aportes)

A capital contribution is the record of an investor buying a part of an issuance made by the fund.

Investors

Risk profile (suitability)

This is the investor's risk tolerance, assessed through a questionnaire. There are three levels in PERFIS:

  • Low (baixo) — prioritizes capital preservation;
  • Medium (médio) — accepts moderate fluctuation;
  • High (alto) — tolerates high volatility.

Investor level

Level Criterion
General (geral) no specific qualification — the retail investor
Qualified (qualificado) ≥ R$ 1 million in investments, or a professional certification
Professional (profissional) ≥ R$ 10 million, or an authorized institution (bank, insurer, asset manager, fund)
